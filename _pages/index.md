## Learning Theory Circle

We meet **every two weeks on Sunday at 7:00&nbsp;pm Tehran time**. Each session, one person presents one paper, their own work or someone else's, on the theory of learning, followed by open discussion. The talks and recordings are in Persian for now. See the [About](about) page for the format and how to present. Announcements go out on our [mailing list]({{ site.mailing_list_url }}), and recordings live on [YouTube]({{ site.youtube_playlist_url }}).
